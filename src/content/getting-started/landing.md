Docs-Ready is a prebuilt documentation site you can shape around your project. Customize the theme, typography, branding, and layout, then generate a ready-to-run site that's yours to develop and deploy.

- Choose from built-in themes, adjust colors and typography, add your own branding, and select a layout that suits your content. Use the guided onboarding experience to configure your site without starting from scratch.

- No admin dashboard or separate content management system. Pages are written in Markdown and managed through GitHub, keeping your documentation alongside the workflow you already know.

- Use the guided onboarding experience to configure your documentation site. When you're done, download a ZIP containing your customized project and the instructions to run it locally. 

- Docs-Ready is designed to be straightforward to customize and maintain. Your generated site can live in your own repository, fit into your existing development workflow, and grow alongside your project.

- It's free to use under the MIT License. If you build on Docs-Ready, please preserve the original license and copyright notice and consider crediting the project.
